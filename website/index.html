What is Ondas?

Ondas requires no account or network access, it just uses the on-device Apple Intelligence model to host conversations and image generation.

On-device intelligence

Chat with Apple's Foundation Models running locally. These are tiny models and aren't very smart, but it's fun to explore.

Optional Personal Telemetry

Optionally ship open telemetry events to an endpoint of your choice to track sessions, token usage, etc.

iCloud sync

Chats sync you across your devices through iCloud automatically.

Image generation

Ask for a picture and Ondas creates it with Apple's Image Playground model.
