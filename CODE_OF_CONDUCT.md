# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Ondas project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help correct their effects.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment; sexualized language or imagery; insulting or
derogatory comments; personal or political attacks; public or private harassment;
publishing others' private information without permission; and other conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public spaces. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions,
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other participation that does not
align with this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to [kd@keegandonley.com](mailto:kd@keegandonley.com).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, consistent with investigating
and responding to the concern. Do not use the public issue tracker for reports that
would expose someone else's private information.
